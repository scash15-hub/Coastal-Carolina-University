Financing

Finance students study banking, investment management, capital budgeting, and funds management. Finance can be called both the art of and science of managing money. All areas of the economy, businesses, nonprofit institutions, or government agencies, have financial issues to be managed. Finance majors graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (B.S.B.A.). They are prepared to pursue careers as financial analysts, stockbrokers, investment bankers, financial planners, research analysts, financial managers, and bankers.
