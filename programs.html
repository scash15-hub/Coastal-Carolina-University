Business

The business programs at Coastal Carolina University are housed within the E. Craig Wall Sr. College of Business Administration, which is fully accredited by the AACSB International.

Mission:Provide experiential business education focusing on coastal community needs and preparing global leaders

Education

Coastal Carolina University offers educator preparation programs through the Spadoni College of Education and Social Sciences.

Mission:Prepare inclusive, reflective educator practitioners via NCATE/CAEP accredited programs.

Science & Mathematics

The Gupta College of Science and Engineering at Coastal Carolina University is the premier destination for a comprehensive STEM education rooted in a liberal arts core.

Mission:Preparing inclusive, reflective educator practitioners through accredited programs.

Health & Human Services

Coastal Carolina University offers undergraduate and graduate degree programs in health and human services through the Conway Medical Center College of Health and Human Performance.

Mission:Personify education via the teacher-scholar model to enhance professional growth, offer rigorous programs, and inspire innovators in collaboration with the community.

Computer Science

The Department of Computing Sciences at Coastal Carolina University offers a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science focused on theory, advanced mathematics, applied software, parallel systems, and algorithms.

Mission:The computing department is to prepare students for careers and graduate studies through a student-centered, ethical learning environment. It focuses on adapting to rapid technological changes while supporting computing education across all university majors.

Recreation & Sport Management

The Department of Recreation and Sport Management at Coastal Carolina University offers academic programs designed to develop foundational skills, knowledge, and practical experience for professional careers or graduate studies in high-demand sport and recreation fields.

Mission:Prepare knowledgeable, effective, and critically thinking entry-level professionals through classroom theory, community-based experiential learning, collaborative research, and internships.

Arts & Humanities

The Thomas W. and Robin W. Edwards College of Humanities and Fine Arts serves as the intellectual heart and cultural center of Coastal Carolina University.

Mission:The program is dedicated to fostering critical thinking, creative expression, and ethical engagement.

Nursing & Healthcare

The Department of Nursing and Health Sciences at Coastal Carolina University advances healthcare through education, research, clinical engagement, and community collaboration.

Mission:To prepare graduates who can provide evidence-based, comprehensive nursing care that is scientifically based, caring, and respectful of diverse individual needs, recognizing the value of a comprehensive baccalaureate education and a sound liberal arts foundation

Financing

The Department of Finance and Economics at Coastal Carolina University delivers applied, professionally connected education to prepare students for careers in finance, wealth management, banking, and economics.

Mission:To develop technically skilled, ethically grounded, and professionally prepared graduates ready to serve businesses and communities through sound financial decision-making. This is achieved through a rigorous blend of experiential learning, academic excellence, and active community engagement.
