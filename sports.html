Football

Coastal Carolina University's football team, the Chanticleers, competes in NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) as a member of the Sun Belt Conference.

Baseball

Coastal Carolina University's baseball program is a prominent NCAA Division I powerhouse competing in the Sun Belt Conference out of Conway, South Carolina.

Soccer

Coastal Carolina University features active NCAA Division I men's and women's varsity soccer programs as well as competitive club soccer opportunities.

Volleyball

Coastal Carolina University fields competitive NCAA Division I women's indoor and beach volleyball teams that compete in the Sun Belt Conference.

Basketball

Coastal Carolina University fields NCAA Division I men's and women's basketball teams that compete in the Sun Belt Conference out of the HTC Center in Conway, South Carolina.

Softball

The Coastal Carolina Chanticleers softball team represents Coastal Carolina University in NCAA Division I as part of the Sun Belt Conference.

Golf

The General James Hackler Course at Coastal Carolina University is the Grand Strand area's only university-affiliated golf course, located adjacent to the main campus in Conway, South Carolina.

Lacrosse

Coastal Carolina University features both an NCAA Division I women's varsity lacrosse program and active student-led men's and women's club lacrosse teams.

Hockey

competitive men's club ice hockey team known as the Chanticleers (or the Chants) that competes in the Atlantic Coast Collegiate Hockey League (ACCHL).
