Freshman Requirements

To apply as a freshman to Coastal Carolina University, you must complete the online application and submit a $60 nonrefundable application fee alongside your official or unofficial high school transcripts.

Learn More

Transfer Students

An applicant is considered a transfer student at Coastal Carolina University (CCU) when they have attended another regionally accredited post-secondary institution in any capacity after completing high school.
